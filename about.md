This is my blog. I occasionally write about things related to Linux, Ubuntu,
the terminal, KDE, Rust and TypeScript. At work, I mostly use TypeScript.

In my spare time, most of the code I write is Rust, but sometimes I also write
Elixir and TypeScript. In the past, I’ve also written a lot of Elm and Ruby. On
this blog, there’s [a couple of posts]({% link category/elm-logo.md %}) about
one Elm side-project in particular: [`elm-logo`][elm-logo].

I’m a contributor to [`gitui`][gitui] and [`gitoxide`][gitoxide].

I’ve also got a second blog at <https://cruessler.github.io/> where I
occasionally post performance analyses related to [`gitoxide`][gitoxide].
